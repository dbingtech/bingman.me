How I work

My process wasn’t learned in a course first — it came from classrooms, help desks and production sites.

  1. 01

    Listen before drawing

    Find out what someone is actually trying to get done before touching anything.

    Learned in IT support
  2. 02

    Rehearse, adjust, repeat

    Hear what isn’t working, fix it, and run it again — small iterations beat big reveals.

    Learned directing bands
  3. 03

    Show the reasoning

    Every decision comes with the options I weighed and why one won.

    Learned in the classroom
  4. 04

    Build it to prove it

    I prototype in HTML/CSS when it’s faster than explaining, and work side by side with developers.

    Learned at BDI
