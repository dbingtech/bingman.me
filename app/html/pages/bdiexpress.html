Challenge

This redesign was driven directly by executive feedback. The CEO described the existing BDIExpress site as “messy and cramped together… amateurish or cartoonish,” and challenged the team to modernize both the visual language and the underlying experience — including introducing AI-powered search. As the primary stakeholder for this initiative, the CEO’s feedback shaped both the design direction and the success criteria for the redesign. Beyond the visual refresh, the homepage needed to do three things: communicate BDI’s value proposition and identity, give users a clear next step (partner with BDI or browse the catalog), and surface the differentiators, contact options, and common self-service tasks that mattered most to customers.
